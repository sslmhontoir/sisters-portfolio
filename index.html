FRAME 002 — FEATURED WORK

My Work

Plastic Tides
FINAL MAJOR PROJECT · 2026 · 2 min 27 secs · Stop motion / 2D / reverse applique
🏆 Winner — Future Generations Award, Cardiff Metropolitan University, 2026

The animation concerns the growing issue of plastic within our oceans and the impact that plastic has on marine life. Living in a seaside town, and seeing the effect that discarded plastic has had on our oceans, beaches and wildlife, is what inspired me to explore the topic of plastic in the oceans. A combination of stop motion and 2D animation was used to tell the story, as well as a fabric technique called Reverse Applique, which involves cutting out sections from multi-layered panels of fabric to reveal the layer below.

My final major project, made across my last year at Cardiff Metropolitan University.

FRAME 003 — ABOUT

Behind the work

I'm an animator combining a foundation in health & social care with a growing craft in animation, using character and story to explore nature, wellbeing and environmental awareness.

A background across Health & Social Care study and a year of Paramedic Science gave me a close, practical understanding of care, emotion and behaviour - the same instincts that now shape how I build a believable character performance on screen.

I'm currently looking to build on that foundation and would love to bring both my craft and my subject matter into a studio environment.
